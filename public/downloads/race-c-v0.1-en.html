The 24-Space Race C v0.1

An original Luozhuo teaching variant · Two players · Choose one of two dice · Human playtesting pending

Save this page for offline use, or use your browser’s Print command for the board and complete rules.

Prepare a complete set of materials

You need two players, one distinguishable pawn each, two ordinary six-sided dice, and a board numbered consecutively from 1 to 24. Numbers determine adjacency. You can arrange four rows of six in alternating directions, or draw another clearly ordered route. Mark start 0 outside the board before space 1.

There are exactly four special connections: ladders 3 → 11 and 8 → 17; snakes 14 → 4 and 22 → 12. All other spaces are ordinary. Mark both endpoints and directions clearly. Put both pawns at 0 and choose a first player randomly, for example by a coin toss, then alternate turns. There are no tokens, cards, supplies, or round limit. You do not need to roll a 6 to start.

Make one move on your turn

Roll both dice together. Choose the value on one die and advance your pawn that many spaces in increasing number order. Ignore the other die. Doubles still give only one move. Do not add the values, reroll, or take an extra turn for a 6. You may examine both destinations before choosing.

If your selected result would take you beyond 24, stay where you began the turn. Do not move or activate any space; end the turn. You may choose an overshooting result even if the other die allows movement. If both results overshoot, stay and end the turn.

Check transfers only after landing

Without an overshoot, complete the selected number of steps first. Landing on 3, 8, 14, or 22 then transfers the pawn to 11, 17, 4, or 12 respectively. Passing these spaces does nothing. Landing at a ladder top or snake tail does not transfer backward. None of this board’s four destinations is another trigger, so resolution then ends the turn.

For example, from 6 choose 2: reach 8 and climb to 17. From 6 choose 6: pass 8 and end at 12 without climbing. From 20 choose 2: reach 22 and slide to 12. Both pawns may occupy one space. Passing or landing on another pawn never blocks, pushes, or captures it.

Reach 24 and end immediately

The first player to reach exactly 24 wins, ending the game immediately without completing the round. From 20, choosing 4 wins. From 23, choosing 2 overshoots and leaves you still. Only one player moves at a time, so simultaneous arrival and its associated tie do not occur in this version.

These instructions use choice version C. Setup needs no single-die procedure, reroll tokens, supply rule, or cooperation deadline from the earlier experiments. Use the materials above to prepare and begin.
