# Income and production: does the money arrive in time?

“Receive income each round” leaves a decision unstated: before the action or after it? Similar amounts and frequency can still leave a player unable to pay when payment is due.

## Two rounds, income before action

This original solo phase starts with 0 coins, income level 1, and 0 points. Everything is public. A sufficient coin supply pays income and receives spent coins; it never runs out.

Play two rounds. Each round, first receive coins equal to the current income level, then take one action. Choose among: pay 1 coin to upgrade permanently from income level 1 to 3, at most once in the phase; pay 3 coins to deliver for 5 points, repeatable; or do nothing. Pay the full cost immediately. There are no loans.

End after the second round's action. Record points and remaining coins. Coins do not score, and there is no extra income payment or third action.

## Turn one coin into an affordable delivery

In round one, receive 1 coin and spend it on the upgrade. You have no coins and income level 3. In round two, receive 3 coins and spend them on delivery. End with 5 points and no coins.

Without upgrading in round one, you have only 2 coins after round two's income, insufficient for delivery. The maximum over two rounds is 5 points. Here, the upgrade makes a later payment possible. It does not establish that buying upgrades early is always best regardless of deadlines and costs.

## Move only income to after the action

Reset the state. Change only the round order to “action, then income.” Keep two rounds, two income payments, and one action per round. An upgrade changes the income level immediately when performed.

| Moment | Income before action | Income after action |
|---|---|---|
| Round one | Receive 1, upgrade, keep 0 | No money, so do nothing; then receive 1 |
| Round two | Receive 3, deliver, score 5 | May spend 1 upgrading, then receive 3; no action remains for delivery |
| End | 5 points, 0 coins | 0 points, 3 coins |

Doing nothing in round two on the right instead leaves 2 coins and still 0 points. Neither legal choice delivers before the deadline, so this version's maximum is 0. More coins at the end do not mean that work requiring an action has already happened.

Although the number of income payments is unchanged, upgrade timing can change their actual amounts. This is not merely a shift of identical payments along a timeline. Track the resulting upgrade and payment opportunities as well.

## Income, conversion, and trading describe different rules

Receiving scheduled coins from the supply is income. Spending them on an upgrade or delivery effect is a cost and conversion. This example has neither negotiated terms between players nor changing market prices.

Specify an income trigger, recipient, amount rule, and source separately. Here, the upgraded payment comes from the supply, not another player's holdings. Making players pay one another would change the resource flow and require another rule.

## Check: does a third round make delivery possible?

Keep income after actions but extend the phase to three rounds. Do nothing and receive 1 in round one. Upgrade and receive 3 in round two. Deliver for 5 points in round three, then receive 3. **End with 5 points and 3 coins.** Round three's income still follows delivery; it cannot be used retroactively to pay the earlier cost.
